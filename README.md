## Chainsaw Man is deeper than it looks {#title}

**Chainsaw Man** is a manga series originally created by mangaka **Tatsuki Fujimoto** for **Shounen Jump**.
On the surface it might be easy to dismiss the series as shallow battle shounen but there are a few reasons why it stands out and these are a few of them: 
- Character Depth
- Use of Symbolism
- Dynamic character interactions
- Genuine Character Growth

A lot of this misunderstanding is also present in the titular main character, **Denji**.
Denji may come across as just a hormonal teenager who is lusting after girls and that aspect is used for comedy but it is repeatedly shown that he is more than that. 
> His yearning for intimacy is a result of the fact that he never had any growing up outside of his pet dog.
> He shows a lack of common sense due to his upbringing but can be surprisingly mature when it matters most.
> He is able to show consideration when others are hurting.
> He can empathize with someone who had just attacked him and reflect on it.
> He is able to make hard decisions for himself when it matters most.
~~He does still act very hormonal though.~~

==Chainsaw Man is a deeper story than people may realize==
